# CARVIO — Job Search Reset | 14-day organic growth sprint

Status: launch kit, not published. Brand account only. No paid spend approved.

## Promise and truth in advertising
**7 minutes to organize your job search** is a guided challenge, not a guaranteed result or claim that CARVIO retrieves job listings. CARVIO provides outbound routes to LinkedIn, Indeed, Google and company career pages, plus application tracking. Do not claim automatic LinkedIn aggregation, guaranteed job discovery, or measurable time saved without evidence.

## North-star
New users who complete a search, open a source and save an opportunity within seven days; distinguish each event and do not assume the saved opportunity came from the search. Secondary: 7-day return rate, attributed registrations, page followers.

## Operating rules
- Publish only from CARVIO brand pages, never the founders' personal accounts.
- No unsolicited bulk DMs, purchased followers, fabricated testimonials or spam.
- Ask community moderators before promotional posts.
- Use authentic screen recordings, not synthetic product UI.
- Do not promise 100 beta users or imply a program exists until staffed.
- Replies should add practical value and disclose CARVIO affiliation.

## QA gate before launch
1. Open each UTM link and confirm the app loads and query parameters do not break navigation.
2. Sign in with a test account; run standard and broadened search; open one source; save a test opportunity; inspect Supabase events.
3. Confirm mobile CTA visibility and login/signup friction.
4. Confirm tracking is for authenticated sessions only; label unknown attribution explicitly.
5. Check social profile descriptions, pinned posts, and contact/feedback link.

## Daily reporting
For each channel, record impressions, post engagements, outbound clicks (if provided), attributed signups, unique active authenticated users, search_submitted, search_opened_by_source, opportunity_saved, and 7-day returns. Record each metric's source and whether deduplicated. Never derive people counts from request or heartbeat counts.

## Stop/go decisions
After 7 days, prioritize channels by activated users rather than impressions. If source clicks are high but saves low, improve product workflow before paying for reach. If traffic is high but registrations low, improve onboarding. Only consider a small paid test after valid event instrumentation and evidence of activation.
